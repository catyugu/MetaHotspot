## 1. Noncommuting operator actions

We study x' = -(A0 + mu1 A1 + mu2 A2)x + Bu, with 48 states and two ports.
All three matrices are symmetric positive definite, but they are dimensionless
algebraic matrices, not necessarily cell-centered thermal M-matrices. The
individual spectra, input vectors and diagonal parameter coefficients remain
fixed across rotation amplitudes 0, 0.08, 0.8. A low-rank rotation yields low-rank
commutators despite full-rank parameter differences; dense rotations are a
negative structural control. Two final ROM orders (10, 14) share a six-vector
frozen-response core. The goal is to isolate additional subspace choice.

Candidates are R_s Ai Aj R_s B and their reversed counterparts, with
R_s=(Astar+sI)^-1 and s in {0.1,1,10,100}. The filtered commutator, ordered-action,
and symmetric-action variants are charged the identical action bank: 96 column
solves including the shared frozen core, 96 operator-column actions and 24 matrix
factorizations. A further-frozen-snapshot baseline uses the same 96 column solves
but 48 factorizations and no extra operator actions. Thus only the first three
have exactly matched primitive budgets; both counts and wall times are reported.
For zero commutators the unmodified frozen snapshots provide rank completion.

An independent comparator is the top eigenspace of the symmetric generalized
controllability/observability Gramian. Its conservative input scale is recorded
and selected before any test, to ensure contraction of the generalized Lyapunov
iteration. Tests always use the ORIGINAL unscaled operator family. This is not
BIRKA; it is not cost-matched to the snapshot methods. Failure to outperform it
is nevertheless relevant. Full published BIRKA, optimized nice selections and
low-rank envelope-system baselines are outside this first screen.

Validation uses 18 new frequency/parameter pairs and two 24-segment input paths,
one forward and one reversed, with segment lengths 0.005, 0.05 and 0.5. Within
segments, exponentials and phi functions are evaluated exactly by symmetric
eigendecomposition, at three observation points. Homogeneous path reversal with
initial state B[:,0] separately isolates order effects (no varying forcing).
The commuting control has zero homogeneous reversal effect up to roundoff.
State and port-output errors, rather than commutator norm alone, decide utility.

Predeclared continuation gate: a roughly twofold error reduction over the best
applicable control on most noncommuting cases at the same order, with acceptable
recorded offline cost. A lower diagnostic rank alone is not success. No theorem
that commutator complexity controls ROM order has been established.

## 2. Shared positive Gram-factor feasibility

A data-only SISO problem is generated by an 8-state SPD pencil
H(s,mu)=b^T(sI+K0+mu K1)^-1 b. Fits use only s, mu and sampled H, never the full
matrices, exact latent responses or validation responses. Both fitted methods
have K0=L0 L0^T+1e-6 I, K1=L1 L1^T and C=I. Two target ranks (2,4), noiseless and
1e-4 relative-noise data, and two 24-point layouts (6-by-4 grid versus correlated
mu=s/10) are tested. Holdout has 180 independently generated off-grid points.
The correlated layout deliberately cannot identify all pencil coefficients.
An exact two-state indistinguishability counterexample is tested separately.

The proposed primitive fits shared low-rank PSD Gram factors through

```
E = Y.T @ Y
G0 = Y.T @ K0 @ Y
G1 = Y.T @ K1 @ Y
diag(s) E + G0 + diag(mu) G1 = ones @ h.T
Y.T @ b = h
```

It uses analytic Jacobians, checked against finite differences. Full latent-row
rank is required to turn small Gram residuals into good equations; latent
condition and actual equation defect are reported rather than assumed away.
This is a fixed-rank NONCONVEX feasibility prototype. It is not the complete
convex-kernel-completion/rank-revelation algorithm proposed in discussion.

The direct positive-pencil least-squares control uses the same pencil structure,
data, initial pencils, two starts and 200 function-evaluation cap per start.
The kernel method has additional latent variables and a different residual, so
an equal evaluation cap does NOT mean equal floating-point work. Total times,
evaluations, variable counts, optimization status and all-start costs are saved.
Each method selects a start only by its own training objective, never holdout.
The kernel result is not silently polished with direct transfer fitting.

Predeclared continuation gate: kernel fitting should offer a reproducible
accuracy, initialization or cost advantage over the direct positive-pencil
control on identifiable grid data. Merely being passive is not an advantage,
since the control is equally passive. Correlated-data interpolation is not
identifiability. No global minimum-rank or global optimizer claim is made.

## 3. State-family spectral cubature

Positive nonlinear harmonic edge conductances are generated on square graphs
with nx=12,24,48 and grounded boundaries. A fixed six-mode smooth basis is used.
Endpoint conductivities are positive exponentials of local reduced temperature;
three material slopes and mild/strong nonlinearity regimes are tested. They are
synthetic constitutive laws, not calibrated material data. The state domain is
[-1,1]^6. The operator is Kr(q)=sum_e g_e(q) a_e a_e.T.

Budgets are 12,24,48 edges. All methods have the SAME 48 candidate states. The
new method chooses nonnegative weights with a finite-pool spectral cutting-plane
exchange. It starts with coordinate and pairwise directions, finds the largest
relative generalized eigenvalue violation, and adds two worst-state directions
per round (up to ten rounds). Each round can reselect the support by a positive
greedy/NNLS step, followed by LP minimax reweighting. The best round is selected
on the training pool only. This is a prototype, not a new convergence theorem.

Strong controls:

* Positive greedy cubature of ALL whitened stiffness entries on the same pool.
* The SAME support from that control, reweighted by spectral minimax exchange.
  This separates a new-support benefit from merely changing the loss function.
* Nominal-leverage selected edges, with NNLS weights fit on the same full pool.

Holdout comprises 192 independent interior states and all 64 vertices. No vertex
or holdout state is used to choose edges or weights. The maximum generalized
spectral error and force dual-energy error are measured. Passing these finite
checks does NOT prove the state-domain-wide inequality in the research proposal.
In particular there is no proof between sampled states, no control of dg/dq,
no certified nonlinear trajectory, and no maximum-temperature guarantee.

Online timing evaluates 64 new states, three repetitions, and includes selected
endpoint temperatures, material coefficients and matrix assembly. It never
computes all edges for a sampled method. The comparator is the full evaluation
of the ALREADY REDUCED operator, not a full PDE solve. Offline pool, feature,
search and fitting costs are charged; break-even query counts are reported.
Grid construction is common setup and recorded separately. Dense feature bytes
and online edge-data estimates are not full process-memory comparisons.

Predeclared continuation gate: at larger grids and 24/48 edges, seek <=5 percent
held-out spectral error, material online savings, and a substantial improvement
over fixed-support minimax, not just over a weak random control. A small support
is not success if it misses the same-domain corner states. A universal result
for arbitrary positive edge laws is impossible without additional structure.

## Interpretation

These screens test concrete algorithmic ingredients under bounded budgets.
Negative results reject the tested construction, not every possible algorithm
in the research direction. Positive results would still need broader baselines,
a principled complexity/convergence result and independent physical validation.
No shared branch is merged and no previous experimental history is rewritten.
